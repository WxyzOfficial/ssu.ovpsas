About the Office

Office of Student Affairs and Services

The Office of Student Affairs and Services (OSAS) of Samar State University is committed to the holistic development of students by providing programs and services that support their academic, personal, and social growth.

OSAS oversees student organizations, activities, welfare programs, guidance services, and student concerns. This management system empowers OSAS personnel with accurate, timely information to serve students efficiently and transparently.

  • Centralized student records from OSAS forms
  • Secure, role-verified administrator access
  • Real-time monitoring of office transactions
  • Complete audit trail of administrative actions
  • Data-driven reports for decision-making
